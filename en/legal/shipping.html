Shipping & Service Info

At Al Shafei Steel Company, we're committed to delivering our industrial machines safely and efficiently to our customers in Egypt and across the Arab world. Below is everything you need to know about shipping, delivery, and after-sales service.

Shipping Regions

We ship to all governorates of Egypt and to 17 Arab countries including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Libya, Tunisia, Morocco, Algeria, Sudan, Yemen, Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, and Palestine.

Delivery Times

  • Inside Egypt: 3 to 7 business days depending on location
  • Arab Countries: 7 to 21 business days depending on destination and customs procedures
  • Custom or large production lines may require additional time — our team will inform you when confirming your order

Packaging & Safety

All machines are professionally packaged using protective materials to ensure they arrive in excellent condition. Heavy equipment is secured with industrial wrapping and wooden crates when needed.

Installation & Training

Our technical team provides installation and operational training for machines delivered inside Egypt. For international orders, we provide remote support via video and detailed installation guides.

Warranty

All machines come with a standard warranty from the manufacturer. Please contact our team to inquire about the specific warranty terms for your machine's model.

After-Sales Support

We provide ongoing technical support via WhatsApp and phone to help you get the most out of your machine. Spare parts are available and can be shipped directly to your location.
