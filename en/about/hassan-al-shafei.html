About

Hassan Al Shafei is the General Manager of Al Shafei Steel Company. He has 12 years of experience managing and operating industrial equipment and follows the company's daily operations from manufacturing through to delivering the machine to the client.

He makes sure every machine reaches the client ready to run, and that warranty, maintenance and spare-parts service continue after the sale inside Egypt.

Areas of Expertise

Managing and Operating Industrial Equipment

Hands-on experience running industrial machines and tuning their performance.

Manufacturing and Quality Follow-up

Following each manufacturing stage to make sure the machine matches what was agreed.

Delivery and Commissioning

Coordinating machine delivery to clients and following up on start-up.

Maintenance and Spare Parts

Maintenance service and spare parts available locally inside Egypt.

Customer Care and After-Sales

Following warranty matters and answering client questions after purchase.

Cross-Department Management

Organizing workflow and coordinating tasks between company departments.
