How to Choose the Right Powder Filling Machine?

Choosing a powder filling machine depends on the required daily production volume and the powder type (very fine or relatively coarse). Al Shafei Steel machines operate with fully automatic control and ensure high filling accuracy even with powder density variation between batches.

Manufacturing Materials & Warranty

All powder filling machines are manufactured from stainless steel 304 (the internationally certified food-grade standard), resistant to rust and corrosion, with locally available spare parts and maintenance inside Egypt.
