Best Legumes Filling Machine in Egypt 2026 — Types & Prices

Introduction

If you're looking for a legumes filling machine for your factory — whether for beans, lentils, rice, kidney beans, or any grains — you'll find several types with different specifications and prices in the Egyptian market. In this article, we'll explain the available types, the factors that determine price, and how to choose the machine best suited to your production volume and budget.

Types of Legumes Filling Machines Available in Egypt

In general, legumes filling machines fall into 3 main systems:

  • Digital Weighing System (Load Cell): The most accurate and most common, weighing the quantity precisely right before filling — suitable for beans, lentils, and rice.
  • Multi-Head Weigher System: For factories that need high production speed and large daily quantities.
  • Volumetric System: More economical, suitable for small and medium factories that don't need exact gram-level accuracy.

Factors That Determine the Price of a Legumes Filling Machine

The price of a legumes filling machine in Egypt varies depending on several factors — there's no "fixed price" for all machines:

  • Filling System: Digital weighing is more expensive than the volumetric system but more accurate.
  • Number of Nozzles/Weighers: The more filling heads, the higher the speed and price.
  • Manufacturing Material: Stainless steel 304 (the certified food-grade standard) costs more than regular materials but lasts longer and is food-safe.
  • Required Production Capacity: kilograms/hour or bags/hour.
  • Level of Automation: manual, semi-automatic, or fully automatic with a digital control screen.

So the best way to know the real price suited to your factory is to send us your production specs (type of legumes, weight per bag, number of bags needed per hour) and we'll give you an accurate quote instead of a generic number that might not fit your case.

Tips Before Buying a Legumes Filling Machine

  • Make sure the machine is made of food-grade certified stainless steel 304.
  • Ask about the warranty and local availability of spare parts in Egypt.
  • Calculate your actual required production capacity before choosing the number of nozzles, so you don't overpay for capacity you don't need.
  • Ask for a real operation demo video of the machine before purchasing.
