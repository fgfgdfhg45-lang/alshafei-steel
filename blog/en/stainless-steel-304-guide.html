Why Stainless Steel 304 is the Gold Standard in Food Machinery

What is Stainless Steel 304?

Stainless steel 304 is an austenitic steel alloy containing 18% chromium and 8% nickel. This composition gives it exceptional properties that make it the global standard for food processing and pharmaceutical equipment.

Why it's Superior

  • Corrosion Resistant: Withstands acidic foods, high humidity, and cleaning chemicals without degrading
  • Food Safe: FDA-approved and non-reactive with food products — zero contamination risk
  • Easy to Clean: Smooth surface prevents bacterial buildup and supports effective sterilization
  • Extremely Durable: With proper care, machines last 20+ years
  • Heat Resistant: Maintains integrity up to 870°C

304 vs 316: What's the Difference?

Stainless steel 316 adds molybdenum for better resistance to chlorides and saltwater environments. For standard food applications, 304 is the optimal choice balancing performance and cost.

What This Means for Your Factory

A machine built with SS304 means: safe food products, lower maintenance costs, longer lifespan, and easier compliance with food safety regulations.
